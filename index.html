Our Story

About Tummy & Me

We started with one mission — bring the crunchiest fried chicken wrapper experience to Malappuram.

Crafted with Love, Served with Pride

From our humble beginnings in Kondotty, Tummy & Me has grown into Malappuram's favorite fried chicken wrapper destination. Every wrapper is made with the freshest ingredients, our secret spice blend, and a whole lot of love.

We believe great food brings people together — and that's exactly what we do, one crispy bite at a time.

What started as a small kitchen with big dreams has now become the talk of the town. Our passion for perfection drives us to innovate every day — from our signature crunchy coatings to our handcrafted sauces. We don't just serve food, we serve happiness.

Whether you're grabbing a quick bite on the go or sitting down with family, every experience at Tummy & Me is designed to make you smile. Because at the end of the day, good food is meant to be shared, and great memories are made around the table.
