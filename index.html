About Tummy & Me

We started with one mission — bring the crunchiest fried chicken wrapper experience to Malappuram.

Crafted with Love, Served with Pride

From our humble beginnings in Kondotty, Tummy & Me has grown into Malappuram's favorite fried chicken wrapper destination. Every wrapper is made with the freshest ingredients, our secret spice blend, and a whole lot of love. We believe great food brings people together — and that's exactly what we do, one crispy bite at a time.
